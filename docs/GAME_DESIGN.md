# ASHVECTOR: Last Signal

Meridian was a coastal communications and industrial island. An electrical containment failure severed its networks; scavenging signal operators now fight within an advancing electromagnetic Static. The visual language uses desaturated coastal greens, industrial teal, warm concrete and amber signal markings.

The eight POIs are Sable Harbor, Relay Ridge, Glassworks, Slate Quarry, Lowland Array, Northwatch, Tidal Depot and Bracken Row. The playable island spans about 680m per side. Hero buildings have front/back entrances, open side windows and external stairs to rooftops. Each neighborhood mixes close interiors, open lanes and longer sight lines.

A match begins with 35 seconds of warmup. A fictional transport starts a 14-second insertion window. Space exits the aircraft; freefall automatically deploys a canopy near terrain if necessary. Space can deploy/cut above the allowed safety altitude; WASD steers. An insertion resets warmup equipment/loot. Players begin with a Vesper pistol, limited armor, plates, a medical kit and grenades.

The Static follows six server-owned contraction phases. Players outside it take increasing damage. Health recovers after a delay; armor must be inserted manually. Caches and floor loot provide all eight usable firearms, ammunition, armor and supplies. Weapon drops retain ammunition and attachments. Rarity controls presentation and some attachment allocation.

Signal Recovery requires holding a relay position for eight seconds and rewards 400 credits plus temporary reconnaissance. Amber Field Terminals sell armor, ammunition, grenades, scans and the player's chosen loadout. The objective can be repeated in this prototype; balancing is not final.

Elimination shows placement, kills and damage. The last living operator wins; local career totals update once per completed operation. Guest records remain on the current browser only. Solo is the supported mode.
